TechDealz Uploader

A private, operator-controlled application for uploading prepared shopping-deal videos to the operator's TechDealz YouTube channel.

YouTube authorization

The application requests Google's youtube.upload permission. Google consent is used to authorize uploads; this page does not provide public registration or access to the uploader. OAuth credentials are stored on the operator's Mac, not on this website.

Uploads contain the prepared video, title, description and tags. The operator controls the selected channel and video visibility. Access can be removed through Google Account third-party connections.
