TechDealz Uploader Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026.

This policy covers the private TechDealz Uploader application, not the general shopping website or third-party merchants.

Google data and permissions

The application requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload to upload videos to the channel authorized by its operator. It stores Google's access and refresh tokens locally on the operator's Mac. It does not request Gmail, Drive, contacts or browsing-history access.

Use and sharing

Tokens are sent to Google's OAuth and YouTube API endpoints to refresh authorization and authenticate uploads. Prepared video files and their titles, descriptions and tags are sent to YouTube. Upload responses, including video IDs and status, are recorded locally for operational checks. YouTube makes uploaded content visible according to the selected privacy setting.

The uploader does not sell Google user data or share OAuth tokens with advertising services or AI model providers. Shopping-deal content may be processed by separate video-generation services; Google OAuth tokens and upload-response data are not used for that generation. Use of Google user data is limited to operating and troubleshooting the authorized upload workflow.

Storage and retention

Tokens, token backups and upload receipts remain in the local pipeline's configuration and data folders until removed by the operator. Token files use restricted file permissions. They are not published to this website. No indefinite-validity guarantee is made: Google may revoke authorization.

Revocation and deletion

The operator can revoke access through Google Account third-party connections, stop the uploader, and remove local tokens, backups and receipts. Revocation does not delete previously uploaded videos; those can be managed separately in YouTube Studio.

This public page

This page has no sign-in form, analytics script or uploader credential collection. Its hosting provider may process ordinary HTTP request data to serve the page.

Privacy questions can be raised through the existing TechDealz Telegram group.
